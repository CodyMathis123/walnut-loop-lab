Case geometry Measured Oct 25

Base structure: one solid front panel and one solid rear panel,each ¾″ thick, each with two 3×120 fan apertures and one upper window. Six flat rails run front to back: three at the bottom, one at the steel-divider top, two at the top. The rails are 2″ across (left–right) × ¾″ high. X = left/right, Y = height, Z = front (+)/back (−).

Approximate lower fan openings are 122 × 362 mm, with their bottoms 3¼″ above the floor and outside edges 1¾″ from each 20″ end-panel side. Both upper windows are 3½″ from each side, 1½″ from the top, and 8½″ high (calculated 13″ wide). Radiator locations and ports remain independently adjustable.

Structure-first view: the front and back are continuous ¾″ walnut panels, with exactly two through-cut fan openings apiece. Rails run depthwise, not left-to-right.Four lower fan apertures and the matching upper front/rear windows are now modeled as genuine through-openings, with approximate measured sizes. Corner geometry and tight clearances remain unverified.
